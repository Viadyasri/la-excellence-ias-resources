## 2. Weekly Revision Routine

A simple weekly structure can help aspirants revisit information consistently.

- Monday–Friday: Read relevant news and prepare concise notes.
- Saturday: Review the week's important topics.
- Sunday: Attempt MCQs and identify weak areas.

Adjust the schedule according to your study hours and preparation level.

## 3. Monthly Current Affairs Review

At the end of each month:

1. Review your weekly notes.
2. Organise information subject-wise.
3. Remove unnecessary repetitions.
4. Revisit important government initiatives and reports.
5. Connect current events with static subjects.
6. Attempt topic-wise practice questions.
7. Mark difficult topics for further revision.

This makes Monthly Current Affairs for UPSC more useful as a structured revision resource.

## 4. UPSC Prelims Practice Checklist

Before marking a topic as revised, ask yourself:

- Can I explain the topic in my own words?
- Do I understand its background?
- Can I connect it with the relevant static subject?
- Have I attempted related MCQs?
- Do I understand why incorrect options are wrong?

These questions can help improve Current Affairs for UPSC Prelims preparation.

## 6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Collecting too many study resources.
- Making unnecessarily lengthy notes.
- Ignoring static subjects.
- Reading without regular revision.
- Practising questions without reviewing mistakes.

## Final Reminder

Effective UPSC Current Affairs Preparation depends on understanding, retention, and consistent practice.

Build a manageable routine, revise regularly, and focus on the relevance of each topic to the examination.
