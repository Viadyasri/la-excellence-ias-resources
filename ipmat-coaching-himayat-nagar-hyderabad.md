What to Compare
1. Faculty and Teaching Methodology

Quantitative Aptitude
Verbal Ability

2. Mock Tests and Practice

Regular practice and timed mock tests can help students become familiar with the examination environment. Performance analysis can also help identify areas that require additional attention.

3. Study Resources

Look for organized study material that supports classroom learning, revision, and regular practice.

4. Mentorship

Consistent guidance, doubt-clearing sessions, and performance feedback can be useful throughout the preparation journey.

5. Intermediate + IPMAT Preparation

Students beginning preparation during Intermediate may consider an Inter + IPMAT Coaching program. An integrated approach can help them manage academic studies while gradually developing the skills required for management entrance examinations.
