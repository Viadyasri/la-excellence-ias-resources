Degree + UPSC 3-Year Preparation Tracker

A practical planning resource for degree students who want to build their UPSC foundation alongside their undergraduate education.

The purpose of this tracker is not to create an unrealistic study schedule. Instead, it helps students divide their preparation into manageable stages and monitor their progress over three years.

🌱 Final Reminder

A three-year preparation journey doesn't need to be rushed.

The objective is to gradually move from:

Understanding → Practicing → Applying → Revising → Testing

Your preparation should grow alongside your degree.

Consistency matters more than trying to study everything at once.

Build the foundation during your degree. Strengthen it through practice. Keep improving.
