## Why Start During Intermediate?

The objective of an integrated foundation program is not to complete the UPSC syllabus during Intermediate. Instead, it focuses on helping students develop:

- Strong conceptual understanding
- Reading and comprehension skills
- Current affairs awareness
- Analytical thinking
- Time management
- Consistent study habits

These skills benefit students throughout their academic journey.

## What Should Students and Parents Compare?

Before choosing an institution, consider the following factors:

- Academic excellence in Intermediate education
- Experienced faculty and mentors
- Structured UPSC foundation curriculum
- Regular assessments and performance reviews
- Student mentoring and academic support
- Learning environment and discipline

The **Best Inter Colleges with IAS Coaching** focus on both academic success and long-term career development.

## Benefits of IAS Coaching for Intermediate Students
